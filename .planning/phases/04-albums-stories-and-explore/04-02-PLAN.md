Facts verified at planning time:
- Touch rule (SV.OoooOO0): DOWN records pressTime, pauses the progress bar and the zoom AnimatorSet. MOVE sets moveAction when the Euclidean distance from the down point is 100.0 px or more. UP/CANCEL: if (now - pressTime) >= limit (500) or moveAction, resume progress and zoom and consume the event (no navigation); otherwise the event falls through to the reverse/skip half-views, whose click goes to the previous/next item. The halves are two weight-1 views in a horizontal LinearLayout, so they mirror in RTL (right half = previous).
- Ad steps (SV.ooOO): loop i = 1..size, when i % ad_position (3) == 0 insert an ad at index i + inserted; so an ad also follows the last photo when the count is a multiple of 3, and a story of 1-2 photos has none. Only when not ad-free (ours never is) and story.native (default true). The progress bar gets one segment per step, ads included.
